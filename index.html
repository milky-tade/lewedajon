Who We Are

Our Mission

"ለወዳጆ" means "for loved ones" — and that's exactly who we serve. Every delivery we make bridges the distance between you and the people who matter most.

Our Story

LEWEDAJO was founded by Ethiopian diaspora members who understood the frustration of not being able to care for family back home. We experienced the worry of a parent needing medicine, the desire to celebrate a sibling's birthday, and the wish to send something meaningful.

Today, LEWEDAJO is a trusted network of shoppers, delivery professionals, and customer care specialists in Addis Ababa — dedicated to making long-distance love tangible. We are more than a delivery service. We are your hands in Ethiopia.
